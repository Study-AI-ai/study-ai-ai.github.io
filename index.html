    Do I have to donate?

    No. Donations are completely optional. You can continue using StudyAI without donating.

    Will my bank details be shown?

    StudyAI page par UPI ID seedhe display nahi hoti. UPI app payment ke waqt recipient details dikha sakta hai.

    How will I know a payment worked?

    Check the payment status in your UPI app or bank account. Clicking a button or opening a payment app does not itself confirm a successful payment.
